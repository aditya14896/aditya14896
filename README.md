### Hi, I'm Aditya Singh 👋

**AI/ML Specialist Solutions Architect at AWS** — I build **auditable, production agentic AI for high-stakes decisions** in the public sector and education.

My focus is the hard part of applied GenAI: not the demo, but making agentic systems **trustworthy, explainable, and safe to ship** in regulated environments (education, government, healthcare) where every decision needs a reason and an audit trail.

🔭 **What I work on**
* Production agentic AI (Strands Agents, Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore)

* Domain-specific classification with web-grounded reasoning and citations
* Knowledge-graph-grounded RAG for public sector and higher education
